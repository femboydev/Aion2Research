# Community Guide Notes: Brawler Macro and PvE Build

**FemboyDev Field Notes 001-H** · English summary · Original article dated 25 August 2026

> This is an original, source-cited summary, not a full translation or reproduction. The original article and its screenshots remain available at [17173](https://news.17173.com/z/aion2/content/08252026/142211502.shtml).

## At a Glance

The author documented a month of Taiwan Brawler play, including an approximately **885,000 combat-power** character without Genesis Dragon gear. The guide's main themes were Rage uptime, keeping Rampage active during boss damage windows, using Typhoon Flurry as part of the repeating macro cycle, and manually handling blocks and movement.

| Topic | Article's broad finding |
|---|---|
| Damage cycle | Rampage uptime and repeated attacks were central to the author's boss rotation. |
| Macro concept | The author placed Typhoon Flurry in the recurring macro to keep the attack cycle moving while other skills returned. |
| Damage skills | Ascending Blow was treated as a major damage skill; Exploding Fist was valued for both damage and Rage recovery. |
| Passive investment | The author ranked Dominance, Impact Hit and Rampage Amplification highly, with Massive Rage as a Rage-capacity option. |
| Defense | The author recommended manually timing Focused Block and using recovery skills when health became dangerous. |
| Positioning | Preserve frontal attacks when safe, but use movement skills when needed to return to the boss. |

## What to Take from It

The most reusable lesson is about **separating automatic filler from manual decisions**. A repeated macro can handle ordinary attacks, while the player watches Rage, chooses when to start Rampage, uses the burst skills at the right time, and responds to boss mechanics.

The article's skill screenshots also show that skill targets and specialization selections should be read alongside the author's gear, Arcana rolls and encounter context. They are not a universal best-in-slot template.

## Why the Macro Advice Is Historical

The article was published on **25 August 2026**. Taiwan's **26 August** update introduced minimum skill-cancellation timing, changing the behavior of high-speed cancel macros. The **11 November** Taiwan update later changed Rampage's cooldown to 15 seconds and changed its fifth specialization to a short Martial Arts trigger. Those later changes mean the article's exact macro timing and Rampage assumptions should not be copied unchanged.

### Current adaptation for Taiwan

Use the [current Build Sheet](BRAWLER_BUILD.md) rather than copying the old macro sequence directly:

- Put Flurry, Roundhouse and Chain Fist in the basic filler loop; use Typhoon Flurry only if it behaves correctly in your macro.
- Keep Rampage, Ascending Blow, Exploding Fist, Focused Block and movement skills manual.
- Use Ascending Blow or Exploding Fist during Martial Arts when the skill is ready and can land inside the short window.
- Test the macro in the current client. Do not assume an old delay value still works.
