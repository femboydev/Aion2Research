# AION 2 Resources for English-Speaking Taiwan Players

**FemboyDev Field Notes 001-G** · Taiwan server · Resource check: 12 November 2026

English Brawler guides are scarce because Taiwan and Korea received classes and updates on a different schedule from the global launch. The most useful approach is to combine Taiwan's official patch notes with English databases that let you select Taiwan data, then use translated player discussions for rotation ideas.

**Related guides:** [Build Sheet](BRAWLER_BUILD.md) · [Skills](SKILLS.md) · [Arcana](ARCANA.md) · [Gear and Daevanion](GEAR_DAEVANION.md) · [Content Builds](CONTENT_BUILDS.md) · [Research Notes](RESEARCH_NOTES.md)

### Translation tips

- Use your browser's page translation for official notices and forum threads. Keep the original skill name or icon open while comparing translated tooltips.
- For YouTube, turn on captions, select auto-translate to English, and use the chapter timestamps. Auto-translation can mislabel skills, so cross-check names in the English skill database.
- Search the Taiwan forum by the English canonical name and the alternate labels listed in the [skill glossary](SKILLS.md#preferred-names-and-aliases).

## How to Verify a Build Claim

1. **Check the date.** A guide before 11 November may give the wrong Rampage cooldown or specialization effects.
2. **Check the official Taiwan notice.** Confirm changes to damage, cooldowns, specialization options and defensive effects.
3. **Check the English skill database.** Confirm the skill identity, then compare the live Taiwan tooltip.
4. **Check the source type.** A character sample shows what players equipped, not what is best; a forum post shows one player's tested setup, not a guaranteed result.
5. **Test the change.** For macros, use a target dummy and check whether skills are skipped, delayed or spent outside the intended Martial Arts window.

## Sources and Limits

- Taiwan build and Arcana usage snapshots linked here were gathered on **7 October 2026**. They predate the 11 November Brawler update.
- The English databases can use alternate translations and may combine Taiwan and Korea data. This repository's preferred English names are listed in [Skills and Specializations](SKILLS.md).
- Player guides and translated videos are valuable discovery sources, but their numeric claims should be checked against official notices and the current client.
- This page is a resource index, not an official support or server-status page.
