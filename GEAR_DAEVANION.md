# Brawler Gear and Daevanion Progression

**FemboyDev Field Notes 001-E** · Taiwan server · Research reference: 7 October 2026 character data, plus Taiwan updates through 12 November

This page adds gear and Daevanion guidance to the [Brawler Build Sheet](BRAWLER_BUILD.md). Preferred skill names follow [Skills and Specializations](SKILLS.md). Use the [Daevanion Selection Grid](DAEVANION_GRID.md) for marked node targets; for boss, AoE, survival and PvP swaps see [Content Builds](CONTENT_BUILDS.md). Gear and build statistics below are snapshots, not current-market best-in-slot guarantees.

## Quick Progression

| Stage | Focus |
|---|---|
| Early boards | Take nearby skill-level nodes for your core damage loop. Avoid long detours for low-value nodes. |
| Damage foundation | Build enough Precision / Accuracy for the content, then favor offensive stats and useful skill-level rolls. |
| Level 45 PvE board | Prioritize PvE accuracy if missing, then PvE attack, boss attack and PvE / boss damage boost. |
| When dying or losing uptime | Replace low-impact damage nodes or gear rolls with practical defense, healing or resistance. |
| PvP | Use the PvP-focused board and gear profile; do not reuse PvE stat assumptions. |

> **Important:** exact caps and stat breakpoints vary with the target and version. This guide does not invent a universal Accuracy or Critical Hit cap. Check in-game combat results and current encounter requirements.

## Gear Priorities

### Stat order for PvE

An August 2026 community guide recommended this practical order: meet the content's hit requirement, then prioritize **frontal damage**, **Attack** and **Strong Hit**. Treat it as a dated starting point, not an official formula. Brawler's frontal damage identity makes front-attack stats especially relevant when the boss can safely be attacked from the front.

| Priority | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1. Hit consistency | Add Precision / Accuracy until attacks reliably connect against the content you are doing. Do not keep stacking it blindly after misses stop being a problem. |
| 2. Brawler damage | Compare frontal damage, Attack, PvE damage and boss-damage options. Prioritize the stats that actually increase your encounter damage. |
| 3. Skill quality | Prefer useful skill-level or Soul Engraving effects on attacks and passives you use. A skill level that crosses a specialization breakpoint can be more valuable than a small generic stat roll. |
| 4. Execution stats | Combat Speed can improve how a cycle feels, but test it after the minimum cancellation-time update. Do not assume an old high-speed macro or cooldown target still applies. |
| 5. Survival | If deaths or forced downtime are limiting damage, take practical health, defense or resistance rather than a fragile paper build. |

## Daevanion Boards

Daevanion nodes add stats and skill levels. A route can cost more than the target node alone because intermediate nodes must also be unlocked. Plan paths instead of spending points uniformly.

### Board unlocks and roles

| Board | Unlock | Main role in a PvE route |
|---|---:|---|
| **Nezekan** | 12 | Early Combat Speed / Cooldown Reduction board with core Brawler skill nodes. |
| **Zikel** | 20 | Damage Boost / Damage Tolerance board; contains several core attack and passive nodes. |
| **Vaizel** | 30 | Critical Damage Boost / Critical Damage Tolerance board with more Brawler damage skill nodes. |
| **Triniel** | 40 | Multi-hit Chance / Multi-hit Resist board with additional core skill nodes. |
| **Ariel** | 45 | PvE and boss-focused stats, including PvE Accuracy, PvE Attack and Boss Damage Boost. |
| **Azphel** | 45 | PvP Attack, PvP Damage Boost, Status Effect Chance and Status Effect Resist. |
| **Marchutan** | 45 | Season 2 stat board, including Weapon Damage Boost and Endurance-related options. |
| **Yustiel** | 45 | Season 3 defensive / utility stats, including Defense, Block, Accuracy and Max Attack options. |

Board unlock levels, themes and Brawler node contents are from the [Brawler Daevanion database](https://aion2.dvg.cn/en/daevanion_board_list.php?category=%E6%8B%B3%E6%98%9F). Check the Taiwan client for the current server's board availability and point currencies.

### Skill-node priority

Use the [Brawler Daevanion Selection Grid](DAEVANION_GRID.md) for priority skill-node targets and their board coordinates. It marks destinations, not a fixed shortest path.

### Practical routing order

Follow the connected path from the nodes you already own. Prefer a short route that reaches a core skill level and provides useful stats on the way. The exact cheapest route depends on your current board state and point budget.

### Node costs

The general Daevanion reference lists common stat nodes at 1 point, passive skill nodes at 2, active skill nodes at 3, and unique board nodes at 4. The board planner counts the connecting nodes on the route as well as the target. Verify displayed costs in the Taiwan client.
